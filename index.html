    LEARN THE GAME

    How to play Baghchal

    01

    Set the board

    Four tigers begin at the corners. All twenty goats begin off the board. Goats move first.

    02

    Place the goats

    Place one goat on an empty intersection each goat turn. Goats cannot move until all twenty have been placed.

    03

    Move and capture

    A tiger moves along one line, or jumps over one goat into an empty point to capture it. No double jumps.

    04

    Claim victory

    Tigers win by capturing five goats. Goats win by blocking every tiger. The game is a draw on threefold repetition.
